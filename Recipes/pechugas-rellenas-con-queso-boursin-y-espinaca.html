Boursin and Spinach Stuffed Chicken

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Boursin and Spinach Stuffed Chicken is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 pechugas of chicken grandes.
  • 1 cup of espinacas cocidas and escurridas.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1⁄2 cup of pan rallado.
  • Sal, pimienta, parsley picado.
Instructions
  1. 1⁄2 t of cheese boursin. puede sustituirlo por cheese neuchatel, feta, cheese crema or ricotta.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
