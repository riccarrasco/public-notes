Chickpea Croquettes

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chickpea Croquettes is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 1 can of 14 a 16 oz of chickpeas cooked.
  • 1 cup carrot grated.
  • 1 tbsp of parsley chopped.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs.
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  2. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  3. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  4. Cook until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  5. Cook for about 8 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  6. Cook for about 5 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
