Chickpea Muffins

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chickpea Muffins is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lata of 14 a 16 oz of garbanzos cocidos.
  • 1 t. zanahoria rallada.
  • 1 c parsley picado.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1/3 t. pan rallado.
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
