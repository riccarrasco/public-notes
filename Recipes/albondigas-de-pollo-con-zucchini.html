Chicken Meatballs with Zucchini

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Meatballs with Zucchini is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 500 gr of chicken sin piel.
  • 350 gr of zucchini.
  • 1⁄2 onion picada.
  • 1 garlic picado.
  • 2 c parsley picado.
  • 1 egg.
  • Pan rallado.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • Pescado and mariscos.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
