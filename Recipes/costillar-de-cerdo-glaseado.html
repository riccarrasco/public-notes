Glazed Pork Ribs

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Glazed Pork Ribs is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 k of costillar of cerdo.
  • 40 gr of miel.
  • 1 garlic picado.
  • 1 c of oregano seco.
  • 20 ml of zumo of lemon.
  • 60 ml of sillao.
  • 40 ml of vino blanco.
  • 2 c olive oil.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
