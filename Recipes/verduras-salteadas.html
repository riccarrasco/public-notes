Sauteed Vegetables

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Sauteed Vegetables is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 zanahorias.
  • 1⁄2 zucchini.
  • 200 gr of zapallo.
  • 2 potatoes.
  • 1 garlic fresco picado.
  • 250 gr of bell peppers varios.
  • 1⁄2 c of tomillo.
  • 1⁄2 c of romero.
  • 2 c of olive oil.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
