Quinoa Balls

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Quinoa Balls is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 t. of quinua cocida.
  • 2 eggs.
  • 1 t. of pan rallado.
  • 1⁄4 of t. of cheese parmesano rallado.
  • 1 c of olive oil.
  • 2 c of albahaca picada.
  • Sal, pimienta, garlic en polvo (opcional).
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
