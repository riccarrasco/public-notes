Pork Cutlets with Ham and Cheese

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Pork Cutlets with Ham and Cheese is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • Filetes of cerdo delgados.
  • Jamon en tajadas.
  • Cheese en tajadas.
  • 2 eggs.
  • 1 c of maizena.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 1⁄2 c curcuma.
  • Pan rallado.
  • 1⁄2 c of garlic en polvo (opcional).
  • 1 c parsley picado.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
