Russian Steaks or Meatballs

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Russian Steaks or Meatballs is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 600 gr of beef of res molida.
  • 1 garlic picado finamente.
  • 1 c of parsley picado.
  • 1⁄2 c of oregano seco.
  • 1⁄2 c of romero en polvo.
  • 1 egg.
  • 30 grams of pan rallado + pan rallado for apanar.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
