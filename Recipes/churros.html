Churros

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Churros is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 160 gr of harina.
  • 1 c of levadura.
  • 1 c of sal.
  • 250 ml of agua.
  • 1 c (20 gr) of mantequilla.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
