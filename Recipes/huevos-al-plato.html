Eggs in a Basket

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Eggs in a Basket is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 3 potatoes medianas.
  • 150 gr of tocino or pancetta.
  • 4 eggs.
  • 1⁄2 t of grated cheese.
  • 2 tomatos medianos.
  • 1 c parsley fresco picado.
  • Sal, olive oil, pimienta.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
