Crispy Parmesan Chicken

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Crispy Parmesan Chicken is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 4 filetes of chicken no muy delgados.
  • 1 or 2 eggs.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 1⁄2 c of garlic picado or en polvo.
  • Maizena.
  • 1 t of panko sazonado.
Instructions
  1. 1⁄4 of cup of cheese parmesano ensalada for servir.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
