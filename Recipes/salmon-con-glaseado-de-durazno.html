Salmon with Peach Glaze

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Salmon with Peach Glaze is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1⁄4 t. mermelada of durazno.
  • 1⁄2 c salsa picante.
  • 1 c garlic picado.
  • 1 c zumo of lemon.
  • 2 filetes of salmon.
  • Sal, pimienta, aceite.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
