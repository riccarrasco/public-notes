Breaded Fish

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Breaded Fish is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 2 tilapia fillets large.
  • Juice of 1⁄2 lemon.
  • Cornstarch.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of oregano, thyme or parsley chopped.
  • 1 tsp of salt.
  • 1⁄4 tsp of pepper negra.
  • Panko or breadcrumbs.
Instructions
  1. Cut and prep the ingredients into uniform pieces so they cook evenly.
  2. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  3. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  4. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  5. Let it rest for about 30 minutes so flavors settle before serving.
  6. Brown the protein for about 6-8 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  7. Follow this step for about 5 minutes, watching texture and doneness as you go.
