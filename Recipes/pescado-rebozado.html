Pescado Rebozado

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Pescado Rebozado is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 tilapias grandes.
  • Jugo of 1⁄2 lemon.
  • Maizena.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1⁄2 c of oregano, tomillo or parsley picado.
  • 1 c of sal.
  • 1⁄4 c of pimienta negra.
  • Panko or pan rallado.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
