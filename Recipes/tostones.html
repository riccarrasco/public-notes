Tostones

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Tostones is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 1 plantain macho green.
  • 1 tbsp of avocado oil.
  • Water.
  • 1 tsp of salt.
Instructions
  1. Cut and prep the ingredients into uniform pieces so they cook evenly.
  2. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  3. Cook at about 200 DEGREES C for about 5 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  4. Remove from heat and set aside briefly before the next step.
  5. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  6. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  7. Cook at about 200 DEGREES C for about 15 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  8. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
