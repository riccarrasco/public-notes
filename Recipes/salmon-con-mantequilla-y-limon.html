Salmon with Butter and Lemon

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Salmon with Butter and Lemon is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 filetes of salmon.
  • Jugo of 1⁄2 lemon.
  • 1 c of miel.
  • 1 c mantequilla derretida.
  • 1 c of parsley picado.
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
