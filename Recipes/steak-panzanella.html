Steak Panzanella

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Steak Panzanella is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp of sugar morena.
  • 1 tsp of salt.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of pepper negra.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of garlic powder.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of onion powder.
  • 1 tsp of paprika.

Instructions
