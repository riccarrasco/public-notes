Apple Crumble

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Apple Crumble is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 4 manzanas amarillas.
  • Zumo of 1⁄2 lemon.
  • 1 c of maizena.
  • 2 c of miel.
  • 2 c of agua.
  • 50 gr of nueces or pecanas.
  • 50 gr copos of avena.
  • 1⁄4 c canela.
  • 50 gr mantequilla fria en cubos.
  • 25 gr of brown sugar or of coco.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
