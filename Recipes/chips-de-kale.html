Kale Chips

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Kale Chips is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

  • 1 c olive oil.
  • 1 c vinagre of arroz, sal.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
