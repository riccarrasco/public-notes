Homemade Bread

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Homemade Bread is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 180 ml of agua templada.
  • 15 gr of levadura fresca of panaderia or 7 gr levadura seca.
  • 300 gr harina of trigo.
  • 1 c of sal.
  • 15 ml of olive oil or 2 c.
  • Platos of chicken.
Instructions
