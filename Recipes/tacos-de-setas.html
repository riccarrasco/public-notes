Mushroom Tacos

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Mushroom Tacos is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb of setas.
  • 1⁄2 c of chili en polvo.
  • 1⁄2 c of garlic and onion en polvo.
  • Sal, pimienta.
  • 1 c of olive oil.
  • Tortillas of maiz or of trigo.
  • Guacamole.
  • Pico of gallo.
  • Frijoles cocidos.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
