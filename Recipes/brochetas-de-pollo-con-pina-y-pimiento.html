Chicken Skewers with Pineapple and Peppers

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Skewers with Pineapple and Peppers is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 pechugas of chicken en cubos.
  • 1 t of pina en cubos.
  • 1 t mini bell pepper dulce rojo en rodajas.
  • 1 t of bell pepper verde en cubos.
  • 1 t onion roja en cubos.
  • 1⁄2 t. salsa teriyaki.
  • 1 a 2 c cilantro picado.
  • Hojuelas of chile rojo.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
