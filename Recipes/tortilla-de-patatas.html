Potato Tortilla

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Potato Tortilla is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 600 gr of potato.
  • 1⁄2 onion.
  • 5 a 6 eggs.
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
