Stuffed Potato

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Stuffed Potato is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 750 gr of potato harinosa (russet potatoes).
  • 1⁄2 onion picada.
  • 1 garlic picado.
  • 1⁄2 c of comino molido.
  • 1 c of aji colorado molido.
  • 1⁄2 c of oregano.
  • 1/3 t. of pasas.
  • 400 gr beef blanda picada.
  • 1 c of parsley picado.
  • 2 eggs duros.
  • 1/3 t. of aceitunas negras.
  • 1 egg.
  • Pan rallado.
  • Aceite neutro, sal, pimienta.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
