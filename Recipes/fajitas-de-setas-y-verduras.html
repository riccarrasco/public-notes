Mushroom and Vegetable Fajitas

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Mushroom and Vegetable Fajitas pair seasoned air-fried vegetables with a creamy avocado sauce.

Ingredients
  • 1⁄2 red bell pepper.
  • 1⁄2 green bell pepper.
  • 200 g of fresh shiitake mushrooms.
  • 1⁄2 white onion.
  • 1⁄2 tbsp of fajita seasoning.
  • Olive oil.
  • 1⁄2 ripe avocado.
  • 4 tbsp of mayonnaise.
  • 2 tbsp of cream cheese.
  • Juice of lemon, salt and pepper for the sauce.
  • Tortillas of maiz or of trigo.
Instructions

  2. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  3. Brown the protein for about 15 minutes until it develops color and aroma.

  5. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  6. Remove from heat and set aside briefly before the next step.
  7. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  8. Brown the protein for about 4-5 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  9. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  10. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
