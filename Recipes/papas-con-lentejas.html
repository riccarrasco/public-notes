Potatoes with Lentils

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Potatoes with Lentils is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 2 potatoes.
  • 1 cup of lentils cooked.
  • Salt, pepper, oil.
Instructions
  1. Cut and prep the ingredients into uniform pieces so they cook evenly.
  2. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  3. Cook at about 180 DEGREES C for about 15 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  4. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  5. Serve warm and finish with your preferred garnish or side.
