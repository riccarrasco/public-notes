Shrimp Po' Boys

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Shrimp Po' Boys is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb of camarones.
  • 1 c sazonador cajun.
  • 1 or 2 eggs.
  • 1 t of panko sazonado.
  • 1 tomato grande.
  • 2 t of lechuga en tiritas.
  • 4 panes of sandwich.
  • 2 c of salsa tartara.
  • 3 cebollinos picados.
  • Beef of res.
