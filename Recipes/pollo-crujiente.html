Crispy Chicken

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Crispy Chicken is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 500 gr of solomillos of chicken (chicken tenderloins).
  • 1 egg.
  • 70 gr of harina.
  • 70 ml of leche.
  • Jugo of 1⁄2 lemon.
  • 2 t. of hojuelas of maiz sin azucar (corn flakes).
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
