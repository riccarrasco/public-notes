Stuffed Peppers

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Stuffed Peppers is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • For 1 persona: 1 bell pepper.
  • 1⁄2 cup of garbanzos cocidos.
  • 1/8 t of couscous.
  • 1 or 2 aceitunas negras.
  • 1 c grated cheese grueso.
  • 1⁄2 c hierbabuena or menta picada.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
