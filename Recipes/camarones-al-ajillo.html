Garlic Shrimp

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Garlic Shrimp is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 garlic en laminas.
  • 1⁄2 c of olive oil.
  • 1⁄2 c paprika or pimenton dulce.
  • 500 gr of camaron.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 1 c of parsley picado.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
