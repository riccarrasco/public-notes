Garlic Shrimp

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Garlic Shrimp is a quick air fryer recipe with shrimp, garlic, olive oil, and bright citrus notes.

Ingredients
  • 1 garlic sliced.
  • 1⁄2 tbsp of olive oil.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of paprika or sweet paprika.
  • 500 g of shrimp.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 1 tbsp of parsley chopped.
Instructions
  1. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  2. Brown the protein for about 5 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  3. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  4. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  5. Brown the protein for about 3 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  6. Cook until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
