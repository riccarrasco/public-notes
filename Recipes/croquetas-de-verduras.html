Vegetable Croquettes

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Vegetable Croquettes is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 zucchini.
  • 1 zanahoria grande.
  • 1 potato grande or 2 medianas.
  • 1 c parsley picado.
  • 1 egg.
  • 2 or 3 c harina of avena.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
