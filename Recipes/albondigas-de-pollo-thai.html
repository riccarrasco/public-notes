Thai Chicken Meatballs

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Thai Chicken Meatballs is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb pechuga of chicken molida.
  • 1⁄4 t. panko or pan rallado.
  • 1⁄2 c. sal.
  • 1⁄4 t. cheese parmesano.
  • 1⁄2 c. garlic picado or en polvo.
  • 1 c chili en polvo.
  • 1⁄2 c. jengibre rallado or en polvo.
  • 2 c of salsa of chili dulce.
  • 1 egg.
