Pasta Carbonara

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Pasta Carbonara is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 200 gr of pasta pequena.
  • 100 gr of tocino.
  • 1⁄2 onion blanca.
  • 1 c aceite vegetal.
  • 200 ml of leche evaporada.
  • 350 ml of agua.
  • 1 egg or 2 yemas.
  • 1⁄2 c of oregano.
  • Vegetales and recetas vegetarianas.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
