Pasta Carbonara

Pasta Carbonara is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1⁄2 white onion.
  • 1 tbsp of oil vegetal.
  • 200 ml of milk evaporada.
  • 350 ml of water.
  • 1 egg or 2 yemas.
