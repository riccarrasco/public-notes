Chickpea and Spinach Burgers

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chickpea and Spinach Burgers is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 400 gr of garbanzos cocidos.
  • 150 gr of espinaca.
  • 1 garlic.
  • 2 c of parsley picado.
  • 1 egg.
  • 60 gr of cheese crema.
  • Si desea ablandarlas: 1 egg.
  • Pan rallado.
Instructions
  1. 4 c of copos of avena sal, harina.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
