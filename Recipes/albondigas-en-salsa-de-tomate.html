Meatballs in Tomato Sauce

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Meatballs in Tomato Sauce is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 700 gr of beef of res molida.
  • 1 onion.
  • 1 garlic.
  • 2 zanahorias.
  • 1 egg.
  • 2 tomatos pelados and picados.
Instructions
  1. 1⁄2 cup of tomato frito 1⁄2 vaso of vino blanco leche evaporada, pan rallado.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
