Curried Beef, Lentils & Kale Stew

4 servings High protein Low carb

A Michelin-inspired one-pot stew built with beef, soaked lentils, kale, and warming curry spices. It’s designed to be deeply savory, nourishing, and easy to follow on a phone while cooking.

Ingredients

Protein & legumes

  • 1 lb ground beef (85/15 for flavor)
  • 1 cup green lentils, soaked 4 hours
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas (optional)

Vegetables

  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1–2 cups chopped kale (stems removed)
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed (optional)

Spices & liquids

  • 1½ tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)
  • 4 cups beef or vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • ½ cup coconut milk
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley
Step-by-step instructions
  1. Prep first: Dice vegetables, rinse lentils, and chop kale. Keep kale in cold water until later.
  2. Brown the beef: Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add beef and let it brown without stirring for 2 minutes, then break it apart and cook until deep brown.
  3. Build the base: Add onion, carrot, and bell pepper. Cook 6–8 minutes until caramelized. Stir in garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute.
  4. Bloom the spices: Add curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, and cayenne. Toast for 30–45 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add tomatoes & broth: Stir in tomato paste, diced tomatoes, soaked lentils, and broth. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to a simmer.
  6. Simmer: Cook 25–30 minutes until lentils are tender and stew is thickening.
  7. Add greens: Stir in chickpeas (if using) and kale. Simmer 5–7 minutes until kale softens.
  8. Finish: Add coconut milk, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Sprinkle fresh cilantro on top and serve warm.
High-protein, low-carb tips
  • Use 85/15 beef for more richness with fewer carbs than added starches.
  • Skip potatoes and add extra kale or cauliflower rice for a lighter bowl.
  • Serve over steamed greens, shredded cabbage, or a small side of cauliflower rice.
Chef’s notes

Restaurant-style depth: Toast the spices in oil before adding tomatoes. This unlocks the full aroma and gives the stew a more complex finish.

Thickness control: For a silkier texture, blend 1 cup of stew and return it to the pot. Add coconut milk last for creaminess without heaviness.

Resting advantage: Let the stew sit off heat for 10 minutes. The flavors settle and the lentils absorb the broth.

Serving suggestions
  • Garnish with cilantro, lemon zest, and toasted pumpkin seeds.
  • Pair with naan, yogurt, or roasted cauliflower.
  • Top with a spoonful of Greek yogurt or coconut yogurt for richness.
