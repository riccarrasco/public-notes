Crispy Zucchini Sticks

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Crispy Zucchini Sticks is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 zucchini.
  • 1 egg.
  • 1⁄2 c garlic en polvo (opcional).
  • 1⁄2 c of oregano seco.
  • 40 gr pan rallado or harina of almendras.
  • 40 gr of grated cheese.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
