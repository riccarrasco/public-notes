Hearty Chicken & Vegetable Soup

6–8 servings Ready in 50 min Comfort food

A classic chicken soup built for easy weeknight cooking, with tender chicken breasts, potatoes, small pasta, and a colorful blend of vegetables. Made for mobile-friendly reading and simple follow-along prep.

Ingredients

Protein & aromatics

  • 2 chicken breasts (1–1.5 lb)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)

Vegetables & starch

  • 2 medium potatoes, ½-inch cubes
  • 1½–2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 cup small pasta (ditalini, elbow, shells, rotini)

Broth & seasoning

  • 32 oz chicken broth
  • 4 cups water (or extra broth for richer soup)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
Instructions
  1. Sauté aromatics: Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and celery, cook 5–7 minutes until soft. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  2. Simmer soup base: Add broth, water, thyme, parsley, pepper, and bay leaf. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to a simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add chicken: Place the chicken breasts into the pot. Simmer gently for 15–20 minutes until they reach 165°F (74°C).
  4. Add potatoes: Add cubed potatoes and simmer 10–12 minutes until nearly tender.
  5. Add vegetables & pasta: Stir in frozen vegetables and pasta. Simmer 8–10 minutes until pasta is al dente and potatoes are tender.
  6. Finish: Remove the chicken, shred it with forks, and return to the pot. Taste, adjust salt and pepper, remove bay leaf, and rest 5 minutes before serving.
Cooker’s Notes

Make ahead: The soup improves overnight. Cook pasta separately if you plan to reheat leftovers, then add it just before serving so it stays springy instead of soft.

Flavor boost: Add a squeeze of lemon juice or chopped parsley at the end to brighten the broth.

Texture tip: If you want a silkier broth, remove 1 cup of soup, puree it, and return it to the pot.

Optional upgrades
  • Use rotisserie chicken for even faster prep; add shredded meat at step 6.
  • Swap chicken breasts for thighs for richer flavor and more forgiving cooking.
  • Stir in fresh spinach or kale in the final 2 minutes for extra greens.
  • Top each bowl with grated Parmesan and cracked black pepper.
