Air Fryer Omelet

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Air Fryer Omelet is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 eggs.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 2 c of grated cheese.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
  2. Omelette of vegetales: 2 eggs, 1⁄4 of cup of bell pepper rojo picado, 1⁄4 of cup of bell pepper verde picado, 1⁄4 of cup of champinones picados, salt and pepper.
  3. Omelette of jamon and cheese: 2 eggs, 1⁄4 cup of jamon picado, 1⁄4 cup of grated cheese of su preferencia, salt and pepper.
  4. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
  5. Omelette of champinones: 2 eggs, 1⁄4 cup of champinones picados, sal.
  6. Omelette of espinaca: 2 eggs, 1 tablespoon of cheese parmesano rallado, 1⁄2 cup of espinaca picada, sal.
  7. Omelette caprese: 2 eggs, 1 c of albahaca picada, 3 tomatos cherry cortados en 2, 1 c of cheese mozzarella rallado, salt and pepper.
  8. Omelette griega: 2 eggs, 1 tablespoon of cheese feta picado, 1 tablespoon of onion picada, 1⁄4 of cup of espinaca picada, 1 tablespoon of bell pepper rojo picado.
