Cheese Croquettes

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Cheese Croquettes is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 500 gr of potato.
  • 1 egg.
  • Pan rallado.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 200 gr of cheese mozzarella en cuadrados.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
