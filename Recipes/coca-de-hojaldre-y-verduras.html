Puff Pastry Vegetable Tart

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Puff Pastry Vegetable Tart is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 1⁄2 zucchini.
  • 1⁄2 white onion.
  • 1⁄2 red bell pepper.
  • 1⁄2 green bell pepper.
  • 1 carrot.
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil.
  • 1 tsp of dried oregano.
  • 15 ml of honey.
  • 10 ml of vinegar balsamico.
  • 10 ml of soy sauce.
  • 1 puff pastry rectangular.
Instructions

  2. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.

  4. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  5. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  6. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  7. Cut and prep the ingredients into uniform pieces so they cook evenly.
  8. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  9. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  10. Cook at about 165 DEGREES C for about 12 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
