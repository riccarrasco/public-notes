Spanish Muffins

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Spanish Muffins is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 3 eggs large.
  • 125 g of sugar.
  • Zest of 1 orange.
  • 1 tsp of vanilla.
  • 100 ml of neutral oil.
  • 250 g of flour.
  • 100 g of cheese quark or yogurt griego.
  • 100 ml of juice of orange.
  • 1 tsp of levadura.
  • 1 pizca of salt.
Instructions
  1. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  2. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  3. Let it rest for a few minutes so flavors settle before serving.
  4. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  5. Brown the protein for about 5 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  6. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  7. Cook at about 175 DEGREES C for about 12 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
