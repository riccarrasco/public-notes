Madeleines

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Madeleines is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 3 eggs grandes.
  • 125 gr of azucar.
  • Ralladura of 1 naranja.
  • 1 c of vainilla.
  • 100 ml of aceite neutro.
  • 250 gr of harina.
  • 100 gr of cheese quark or yogurt griego.
  • 100 ml of zumo of naranja.
  • 1 c levadura.
  • 1 pizca of sal.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
