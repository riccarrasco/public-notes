Chicken Wings

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Wings is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 700 g of wings of chicken.
  • 1⁄4 tsp of garlic powder.
  • 1 tbsp of soy sauce.
  • 2 tbsp of honey.
  • 3 tbsp of salsa barbacoa.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of romero powder.
Instructions
  1. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  2. Let it rest for a few minutes so flavors settle before serving.
  3. Cook at about 185 DEGREES C for about 20 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  4. Cook until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
