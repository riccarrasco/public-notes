Chicken Wings

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Wings is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 700 gr of alitas of chicken.
  • 1⁄4 c garlic en polvo.
  • 1 c of sillao.
  • 2 c of miel.
  • 3 c of salsa barbacoa.
  • 1⁄2 c romero en polvo.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
