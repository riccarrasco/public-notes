Pumpkin and Zucchini Muffins

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Pumpkin and Zucchini Muffins is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 200 gr of zapallo.
  • 200 gr of zucchini.
  • 100 gr of datiles sin hueso.
  • 1 yogurt natural.
  • 60 ml of aceite suave.
  • 100 ml of leche.
  • 1 c of vainilla.
  • 70 gr of brown sugar.
  • 250 gr of harina integral.
  • 3 eggs.
  • 50 gr of cacao en polvo.
  • 75 gr of harina of almendras.
Instructions
  1. 15 grams levadura en polvo una pizca of sal 75 grams of chispas of chocolate.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
