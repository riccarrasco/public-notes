Empanadas

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Empanadas is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • Masa for empanadas.
  • Relleno of beef molida, chicken or cheese.
Instructions
  1. Preparar las empanadas rellenandolas with beef molida sofrita with salt and pepper, chicken desmenuzado or cheese en tajadas. pincelarlas with egg batido and espolvorearlas with garlicnjoli. cooklas en air fryer a 350 grados f por 10 minutos.
