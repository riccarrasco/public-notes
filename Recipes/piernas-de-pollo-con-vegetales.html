Chicken Legs with Vegetables

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Legs with Vegetables is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 4 piernas of chicken with muslo.
  • 1 cup cut and potatoes.
  • 1 cup sweet potato pelado diced.
  • 1 cup carrot sliced.
  • 1⁄2 onion.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of garlic and onion powder.
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of chili powder.
  • Salt, pepper, herbs italianas.
  • 1 tbsp of parsley chopped.
Instructions
  1. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  2. Follow this step in sequence, keeping texture and doneness cues in mind.
  3. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  4. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  5. Let it rest for about 20 minutes so flavors settle before serving.
  6. Brown the protein for about 20-25 minutes until it develops color and aroma.
  7. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  8. Serve warm and finish with your preferred garnish or side.
