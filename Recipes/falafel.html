Falafel

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Falafel is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lata of 14 a 16 oz of garbanzos cocidos.
  • 1⁄4 onion finamente picada.
  • 1 garlic picado.
  • 1 c cilantro picado.
  • 1 egg.
  • 3 or 4 c pan rallado.
  • 1 c tahini (opcional).
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
