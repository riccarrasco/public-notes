Falafel

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Falafel is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 1 can of 14 a 16 oz of chickpeas cooked.
  • 1⁄4 onion finamente chopped.
  • 1 garlic chopped.
  • 1 tbsp of cilantro chopped.
  • 1 egg.
  • 3 or 4 tbsp of breadcrumbs.
  • 1 tbsp of tahini (optional).
  • Salt and pepper.
Instructions
  1. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  2. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  3. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  4. Add the next ingredients and combine well so everything cooks evenly.
  5. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  6. Cook until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  7. Cook for about 8 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  8. Cook for about 5 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
