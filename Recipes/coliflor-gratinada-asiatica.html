Asian Cauliflower Gratin

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Asian Cauliflower Gratin is a home-style air fryer recipe with clear steps and practical ingredients for everyday cooking.

Ingredients
  • 1 coliflor entera.
  • 1 tbsp of soy sauce.
  • 1⁄2 tbsp of salsa of ostion.
  • 2 tbsp of salsa hoisin.
  • 1 tsp of chili powder.
  • 1⁄2 tsp of salt.
  • 1 tsp of neutral oil.
Instructions
  1. Cook until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
  2. Mix everything well in a bowl until the texture is even and fully combined.
  3. Brown the protein until it develops color and aroma.
  4. Cook at about 180 DEGREES C for about 10-15 minutes until fully done, flipping halfway if needed.
