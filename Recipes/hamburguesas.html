Burgers

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Burgers is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb beef of res molida sin grasa.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • 4 tajadas of cheese cheddar.
  • 4 panes for hamburguesas.
  • Onion en rodajas.
  • Tomato en rodajas.
  • Lechuga or arugula, mayonesa and ketchup.
Instructions
  1. Puede addse garlic and/or onion en polvo, paprika, oregano and/or parsley.
  2. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
