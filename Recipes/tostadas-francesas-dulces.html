Sweet French Toast

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Sweet French Toast is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • Tajadas of pan of molde.
  • 2 eggs.
  • 1/3 t. of leche.
  • 1 c. brown sugar.
  • 1⁄4 c. vainilla.
  • The listed amount of the ingredient noted in the original recipe.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
  2. Placelas en un plato and acompanarlas with rodajas of plantain and fresas, diferentes berries and rociar with miel.
