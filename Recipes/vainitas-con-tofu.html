Green Beans with Tofu

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Green Beans with Tofu is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb of vainitas.
  • 200 gr of tofu firme.
  • 1 c of sillao dulce.
  • 1 c aceite of garlicnjoli.
  • 1 c of maizena.
  • Garlic en polvo, sal, pimienta, aceite.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
