Savory French Toast

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Savory French Toast is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 eggs.
  • 1/3 t. of leche.
  • Sal, pimienta.
  • Pan of molde.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
  2. Hacer 4 tostadas. place encima:.
  3. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
  4. Place en cada tostada una sardina and encima una sarza of onion blanca picada en cuadritos with parsley picado and aji verde picado, sazonada with sal, pimienta, aceite and lemon or vinagre.
  5. Apretar una palta en un bol, add 1 tablespoon of onion blanca picada menudita and 2 tablespoons of bell pepper picado. salpimentar.
