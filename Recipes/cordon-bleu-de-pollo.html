Chicken Cordon Bleu

Air fryer recipe Tuti's recipe

Chicken Cordon Bleu is an air fryer recipe with clear, conversational steps and practical home-kitchen ingredients.

Ingredients
  • 2 pechugas grandes of chicken sin piel.
  • 4 tajadas of jamon.
  • 4 tajadas of cheese suizo.
  • Harina or maizena.
  • 2 eggs batidos.
  • Salt and pepper.
  • Pan rallado or panko.
  • Parsley picado.
Instructions
  1. Follow the same preparation flow: combine ingredients, preheat the air fryer, cook until done, and turn halfway when needed.
