Sat, Sep 26

Scenic canyon drive, chairlift, and Park City

Morning

  • Leave MainStay around 9:00 AM. Follow the saved Salt Lake City to Park City scenic route.
  • Plan: Big Cottonwood Canyon -> Guardsman Pass -> Bloods Lake Trailhead -> Bonanza Flat -> Empire Pass -> Park City.
  • In Big Cottonwood Canyon, use pullouts for canyon walls, creek, and aspen stands. Silver Lake for restrooms.
  • At Guardsman Pass, allow 15-30 minutes for the overlook, fall colors, and a temperature/wind check. This is the main high-point panorama before the hike; conditions can be much colder than Salt Lake City.
  • Hike Bloods Lake from Bloods Lake Trailhead: 2.8 miles round trip with 465 feet of gain. Plan about 90 minutes, including lake time and photos.

Afternoon

  • Drive through Bonanza Flat and Empire Pass after Bloods Lake. Stop at pullouts or trailhead parking. Look for open alpine meadows, aspens, and broad views across the Wasatch; Bonanza Flat is the better stop for meadow/lake scenery, while Empire Pass is the quicker high-elevation viewpoint.
  • Short additional walk: use a brief trailhead wander at Bonanza Flat rather than committing to another destination.
  • Lunch options: Tavolona, immediately beside Pioche Village. Check Saturday lunch hours. Atticus Coffee, Books & Teahouse on Main Street as quick fallback.
  • Take the Crescent or Payday scenic chairlift. $30-50 per person.
  • Recommended hike: take a 60-90 minute out-and-back on the Mid Mountain Trail near the top, turning around in time for the last lift.
  • Check in at Pioche Village Park City, drop daypacks, and head to Park City Mountain.

Evening

  • Park near Historic Main Street. Before dinner, stop at Atticus Coffee, Books & Teahouse, Dolly's Bookstore, and any galleries or shops that interest you.
  • Dinner options: Courchevel Bistro; Grappa for Italian; Riverhorse on Main for steak, seafood, and wild game; Firewood or Purple Sage for upscale American; then Prime Steak House & Piano Bar for steakhouse dining. Target 7:00 PM.
  • After dinner, do a short Main Street walk, then return to Pioche Village for the hot tub.

Thu, Oct 1

Lupine Meadows hike, Teton stops, sunset

Route

  • Full Oct 1 Google Maps route.

Stops in route order

  • Optional: T.A. Moulton Barn at Mormon Row: historic homestead barn with the Tetons behind it.
  • Optional: Taggart Lake Trailhead and the Taggart Lake Trailforks search: a glacial lake framed by the Tetons. NPS reports a construction detour; confirm access.
  • Main hike: From Lupine Meadows Trailhead, hike about 2 miles up the Amphitheater Lake route on Trailforks, then turn around and return, about 4 miles total. This does not reach Amphitheater Lake; look back for broad Jackson Hole and Teton Range views. Check NPS trail conditions first.
  • Optional: Jenny Lake Visitor Center and a short lakeshore walk for Cascade Canyon and Cathedral Group views. Shuttle and kayak rentals ended Sept 30.
  • Optional: String Lake shoreline walk or Leigh Lake walk. String is shallow with close Teton views; Leigh opens toward Mount Moran. Trailforks lake-trail search.
  • Optional: Oxbow Bend Turnout for Snake River reflections and possible wildlife.
  • Optional: Jackson Lake Overlook for a broad lake-and-mountain panorama.
  • Optional: Snake River Overlook for the winding river through the valley.
  • Optional: Schwabacher Landing for beaver-pond reflections beneath the Tetons.
  • Optional: Antelope Flats for open sagebrush, wildlife, and mountain views. Choose only what daylight allows.

Evening

  • Finish at Gravity Haus Teton Village for sunset if timing allows, dinner, relaxation, and check-in.
Fri, Oct 2

Mormon Row sunrise, optional carryovers, Snake River float

Sunrise and core route

  • Oct 2 core Google Maps loop: Gravity Haus to T.A. Moulton Barn at Mormon Row, Antelope Flats, Jackson Hole WhiteWater, and back.
  • At T.A. Moulton Barn at Mormon Row, photograph the historic barns against the Tetons at sunrise. Antelope Flats adds open sagebrush, wildlife possibilities, and broad mountain views.

Optional Oct 1 carryovers

  • If daylight and schedule allow, choose missed stops: Taggart Lake for a Teton-framed glacial lake; Jenny Lake for Cascade Canyon views; String Lake or Leigh Lake for shoreline views toward the Tetons and Mount Moran; Jackson Lake Overlook for the broad lake panorama; Oxbow Bend for reflections and wildlife; Snake River Overlook for the winding river; or Schwabacher Landing for beaver-pond reflections.

Afternoon boat activity

  • Optional guided float: 7-mile Snake River scenic float with Jackson Hole WhiteWater. Listed 1:00 PM or 4:00 PM departures through Oct 11; about 2-2.5 hours round trip. Confirm Oct 2 availability. This is a guided raft float, not a lake kayak rental.
  • Jenny Lake kayak rentals and shuttle service ended Sept 30. Private watercraft require park permits and applicable Wyoming aquatic-invasives inspection.
Return to Gravity Haus Teton Village for dinner and the second night.
