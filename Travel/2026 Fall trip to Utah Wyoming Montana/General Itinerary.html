Fri, Sep 25 Arrive in SLC; drive to Fort Union. MainStay Suites Salt Lake City Fort Union Clear, low near 61 F in Cottonwood Heights. No precipitation signal. Travel clothes plus a light jacket.
Sat, Sep 26 Scenic canyon drive, chairlift, and Park City. Pioche Village Park City Sunny, near 73 F in Park City; colder and windier at Guardsman Pass. Dry. T-shirt, light long sleeve, hiking pants or shorts, hiking shoes. Carry fleece or light puffy, rain shell, beanie, and thin gloves.
Sun, Sep 27 Wildlife viewing, then drive to Lehi. SpringHill Suites Lehi at Thanksgiving Point Partly sunny, near 71 F in Park City; Lehi near 82 F. Shower/thunderstorm chance increases after dark. T-shirt and shorts or hiking pants, plus a light layer and rain shell. Bring a dry change of clothes and insulating layer for water time.
Mon, Sep 28 Workday with first Wasatch MTB ride. SpringHill Suites Lehi at Thanksgiving Point Cool and mostly cloudy: Park City near 61 F; Lehi near 71 F. Showers likely with possible thunderstorms. Trails may be wet or closed. MTB layers plus waterproof shell; treat the ride as weather-dependent and use a non-riding backup if trails are wet.
Tue, Sep 29 Long Wasatch MTB ride after work. SpringHill Suites Lehi at Thanksgiving Point Improving: Park City near 60 F; Lehi near 69 F. Slight shower chance in Park City; otherwise mostly sunny. MTB shorts and jersey, arm sleeves or light long sleeve, gloves, wind shell, and packable rain shell. Take an insulating layer and extra food for the long ride.
Wed, Sep 30 Morning MTB, bike return, drive to Alpine. Salt River Lodge, Alpine, WY Sunny: Park City near 63 F; Lehi near 71 F; Alpine near 61 F. Dry forecast. Same MTB layers as Tuesday. Add warm travel layers for the Alpine evening drive.
Thu, Oct 1 Snake River drive, hot springs, Grand Teton. Gravity Haus Jackson Hole, Teton Village Sunny, high near 67 F near Moran; low near 34 F. Dry. Hiking pants, moisture-wicking long sleeve, fleece, light puffy, waterproof shell, warm socks, waterproof hiking footwear, beanie, and gloves. Pack swimsuit and sandals for Astoria.
Fri, Oct 2 Sunrise, lakes, hiking, wildlife, scenic drives. Gravity Haus Jackson Hole, Teton Village Sunny, high near 69 F near Moran; low near 37 F. Snow/ice possible at elevation. For sunrise: base layer, fleece, puffy, shell, warm pants, beanie, gloves, and warm socks. For hiking: layers that can come off, waterproof boots, and microspikes in the car.
Sat, Oct 3 Enter Yellowstone; visit geysers, lakes, valleys. Corral Villas Yellowstone, Gardiner Mostly sunny in Gardiner, high near 76 F; low near 45 F. Dry. Base layer, fleece, puffy for morning/evening, waterproof shell in the car, hiking pants, waterproof footwear, beanie, and gloves.
Sun, Oct 4 Explore Lamar Valley, hot springs, wildlife. Corral Villas Yellowstone, Gardiner Sunny in Gardiner, high near 80 F; low near 48 F. Lamar Valley may be colder early and late. Dry. Warm layers for the early wildlife drive, lighter layers by afternoon, waterproof boots, and swimsuit for hot springs.
Mon, Oct 5 Choose Beartooth or scenic route to Bozeman. MountainView Lodge & Suites, Bozeman Mostly sunny: Gardiner near 79 F; Bozeman near 78 F. Beartooth can be much colder than either town. Check Beartooth elevation weather and road status before committing. Winter-capable layers: base layer, fleece, puffy, waterproof shell, beanie, gloves, warm socks, and waterproof footwear.
Tue, Oct 6 Easy Bozeman morning, then fly home. Home Sunny, high near 74 F in Bozeman; low near 49 F. Dry. No mountain travel planned. Travel layers and a light jacket.
